📖 Pocket Virology & Biotech Guide

Everything in ViroQuest is based on real biology from Class 12 Biotechnology & 1st-year Molecular Biology! Here are the 4 big ideas behind the games:

🔑 1. Lock-and-Key Cell Entry

Viruses cannot multiply on their own. To sneak inside a human cell, a virus's outer Spike protein ("Key") must fit into a specific Receptor ("Lock") on our cell membrane — like COVID-19's Spike binding ACE2, or HIV's gp120 binding CD4.

🛡️ 2. How Neutralizing Antibodies Work

Vaccines train B-cells to make Y-shaped Neutralizing Antibodies. These antibodies snap onto the virus's outer spikes before it reaches your cell — covering up its "keys" so infection never starts!

⭕ 3. Recombinant Plasmids & Cloning

Instead of growing dangerous live viruses, scientists copy just one outer viral gene into a circular bacterial DNA ring called a Plasmid (with an Origin, Promoter switch, and Kanamycin tag), transform E. coli at 42°C, and check white colonies on a UV DNA gel!

💉 4. Modern Vaccine Capsules

Once we have the pure viral blueprint, we deliver it safely as an mRNA Lipid Bubble (COVID-19), a Harmless Carrier Virus (Ebola Ervebo), a Purified Protein + Alum (Flu), or an Empty Virus-Like Shell (HPV Gardasil/Cervavac)!

🎮 How to Play ViroQuest

🛡️ Mode 1: Cell Defender

Look at the glowing shape inside each falling virus (👑 ⭐ 🟦 🔺 ⬡). Click the matching Antibody Button at the bottom, click the cell receptor, drag across, or press keys 1–5!

🧬 Mode 2: Vaccine Studio

1) Pick a target virus.
2) Choose an OUTER surface gene.
3) Snap on a human/RNA Promoter + high-copy Ori + KanR tag.
4) Pulse 42°C Heat-Shock & click 2 White Colonies to run the UV Gel!
5) Pick a Delivery Capsule & print your Certificate!

🕵️ Mode 3: Virus Detective

Click a Virus Card on the left and its matching Clue Card on the right — OR drag a glowing laser cable straight across! Stuck? Click 💡 Hints in the top bar.

🎓 About ViroQuest

ViroQuest is a self-contained, 100% offline educational game created for College Open Day outreach on Virology, Host–Virus Interactions & Modern Vaccine Design.

🧬 Curriculum Alignment

Tailored for Class 12 Biology / Biotechnology and 1st-Year Undergraduate Life Sciences — turning NCERT/undergraduate concepts like receptor-mediated entry, recombinant plasmids (Ori, KanR, restriction sites), blue-white colony selection, agarose gel electrophoresis, and mRNA/VLP vaccines into hands-on play.

Built with pure HTML5 Canvas, SVG, CSS3, and procedural Web Audio API synthesis — runs offline on any laptop with a single click.
